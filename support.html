Common questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to fire the cannon at that spot. Hold your finger down to keep firing. Knock every piece off the platform before you run out of balls.

What do the boosters do?

Ball Storm gives you free rapid fire for 3 seconds. Mega Shot fires heavy balls that crash through walls. The Zap Wand clears a box and its neighbours. The Bomb unlocks at level 10 and blasts a whole section.

How do I get coins?

Clear levels. Hard levels pay double and Super Hard levels pay triple. Spend coins in the Shop on boosters, lives, avatars and frames. There are no real-money purchases and no ads.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone or iPad Settings, then open the Leaderboard tab in the game. Your highest level is sent to Game Center automatically.

What are Treasure Dig and Bus Rush?

Two bonus games on the home screen. Shovels and bus tickets come from daily rewards and every 10 levels.

I ran out of lives.

A new life arrives every 30 minutes, up to 5. You can also refill them with coins.

Will I lose my progress?

Your save lives on your device. Deleting the app erases it. Use Settings → Backup & Restore to copy your save code and restore it on another device.

Can more than one person play on the same device?

Yes. Settings → Save Profiles has three separate profiles, and each can be reset at any time.

The game feels stuck or looks wrong.

Close the app fully and reopen it. If a problem persists, email me with your device model and what you were doing, and I'll fix it.

Reporting a bug

The more detail the better: what happened, what you expected, which level you were on, and a screenshot if you have one.
