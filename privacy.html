Privacy Policy

Box Smash · Last updated 23 October 2026

Box Smash does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This includes:

  • Your level, coins, lives and boosters, for each of the three save profiles
  • Avatars and frames you have unlocked
  • Your player name, stats and settings (music, sound, vibration)

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, it is not readable by us, and it is not shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Backup codes

Settings → Backup & Restore shows a code that contains your save. The code is only shown on your screen; the game does not send it anywhere. If you copy it or share it, that is entirely your choice.

Internet access

The game itself makes no network requests and runs fully offline. The only exception is Apple Game Center, below.

Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game sends your highest level to Apple's Game Center leaderboard so you can compare it with other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive or store your Game Center name or ID. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device Settings.

Purchases

There are no real-money purchases. Everything in the shop is bought with coins earned by playing.

Third parties

No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no social media integrations and no cloud services. The only outside service is Apple Game Center, used for the leaderboard.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Your rights

There is no data for us to show you, correct, export or delete, because we never receive any. The save data on your device is entirely under your control, and each save profile can be reset from Settings → Save Profiles.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
